📊 Market Making Engine market_making.py

Three quantitative models power the market-making engine: Avellaneda-Stoikov for optimal spread quoting, Almgren-Chriss for execution scheduling, and Glosten-Milgrom for adverse selection pricing. The MarketMakingEngine orchestrates quote generation, inventory management, and execution scheduling.
